This is the practical member's handbook — the script and reference for running a gathering. For the full guide to the fellowship (mission, meetings, renewals, history), see the companion Member Guide & Handbook booklet.

Each Fourth Monday

Order of Service

Our evening follows a consistent rhythm — familiar enough to feel like home, open enough to hold whatever arises.

6–7
Potluck Dinner

Arrive, share food, and connect informally. First-timers never need to bring a dish — just come as you are.

7pm
Welcome Circle Opens

We come together as a group and open the circle with four elements. The facilitator (or any member) can read the passages below aloud.

Welcome Circle — Read Aloud
1. Mission Statement

Facilitator reads aloud:

"Our mission is to support men in the quest for lives of compassion, integrity, responsibility, and balance. We are a caring, intentionally diverse brotherhood, committed to social justice, and offering opportunities for intimate sharing, personal growth, and spiritual deepening. Our goal as a fellowship is that after each gathering, we can say to ourselves that we know a little more about ourselves as men, sons, and brothers."

2. Lighting of the Chalice

A chalice is lit to mark the opening of our shared space. Suggested words (customize as you like):

"We light this chalice to mark the beginning of our time together — a symbol of the light we bring to one another, and the truth we seek in community."

3. Gong of the Bowl

Ring the bowl once. Allow the sound to fade into silence — a slow count of ten — before speaking again. A moment of collective stillness; we arrive together before we begin.

4. Confidentiality Statement

Facilitator reads aloud:

"The fellowship is built upon trust that what is shared in ALL the circles is to be held in the strictest of confidence!"

7–8
Circle Check-In

Each man takes a turn to share how he's doing — what's alive for him, what he's carrying or celebrating. No cross-talk. No advice. Just being heard.

8–9
Small Groups

We break into smaller circles to go deeper — sharing responses to questions or themes, supporting one another. After small group time, we return as a full circle.

9pm
Closing Circle

We close with a poem, song, or chant together — then clean up, stack chairs, and put the space back as we found it.

How We Be Together

Norms & Traditions

Our official Gathering Guidelines, verbatim — plus two long-standing practices that round out how we hold the space together.

01
Confidentiality

The fellowship is built upon trust that what is shared in ALL the circles is to be held in the strictest of confidence!

02
The Right to Reticence

Share only as deep as you are comfortable.

03
Use "I" Statements

Tell YOUR story and how YOU feel.

04
Active Listening

No cross talk.

05
Give No Advice

Unless requested, and usually after the gathering.

06
Speak Loudly

So that all can hear and understand your story.

07
Allow Time for Everyone to Share

Before you share a second time.

08
Chants Are Sung Together

Chants are sung three times — the first time robust, the second louder, the third quieter or hummed. The leader often steps up volume on key phrases. Tune out your inner critic and just sing.

09
The Potluck Is Part of the Gathering

Breaking bread together is the first act of fellowship. Come early enough to share the meal. First-timers are never expected to bring food.
